# Terminological Issues to be Addressed

## Dealing with Mass Nouns vs. Count Nouns

Have terms like "dielectric" that represent indefinite extents.

One approach seems to be to have a stuff/thing distinction.

* Stuff: continuous/not inherently countable - for us, a type of material under consideration. Indenfinite extents.
* Thing: a countable object, definite extents
